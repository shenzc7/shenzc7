## I turn complicated work into clear, dependable products.

I'm a product-minded developer focused on software that feels fast, survives unreliable connections, and helps people make better decisions. I enjoy owning the whole journey—from shaping the system and interface to shipping, measuring, and refining it.

- **Product systems:** thoughtful tools for operations, commerce, finance, and teams
- **Resilient experiences:** offline-capable apps across web, desktop, and mobile
- **Useful AI:** automation and intelligence that earn their place in the workflow
